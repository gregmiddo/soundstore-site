Features

What Soundstore does

Point of sale

Fast counter sales with quick keys, barcode scanning, discounts, refunds, lay-by, quotes and a customer-facing screen. Close the day with a full takings report.

Stock and suppliers

Catalogue, serial numbers and warranties, stocktakes, price labels, purchase orders and supplier returns.

Repairs

Book instruments in, track parts and progress, and let customers know the moment it's ready.

Hire and rent-to-buy

Gear and PA hire with bookings, check-out and returns, plus rent-to-buy plans.

Lessons and rooms

Students, timetables, courses and progress, a lesson builder with chords and strumming patterns, and room bookings.

Flyers and promotion

Design flyers, gig guides and gift vouchers in the app, then print or email them.

Customers and enquiries

Customer history, statements, enquiries and follow-up reminders so no sale slips through.

Reports

Takings, profit, enquiries and end-of-year reports, with exports for your accountant.

Multiple counters

Run several PCs in the shop on the same data, with staff sign-in by PIN.
