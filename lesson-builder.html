01 / 05 · The lesson builder

Build a lesson in minutes

Lessons · Library · New lesson

Start blank, from a template, or from a copy of a library lesson, then add parts from the left.

  • Parts sit in Warm up, Learn it, Play it and Wrap up, with the time each starts.
  • Every change saves by itself, with Undo and Redo.
  • Each card says what the student does.

02 / 05 · The lesson builder

Chord charts that get the fingers right

Builder · Chords

About 245 guitar chords in every key, open and barre shapes, plus bass, ukulele and keyboard.

  • Each chart shows which finger goes where, open and muted strings, and the fret.
  • Click chord pictures to build the progression in playing order.

04 / 05 · The lesson builder

The studio layout for big screens

Builder · ⋯ · Open in its own window

On a big screen the builder shows the parts, the run sheet, the part you're editing and the handout side by side.

  • Building for a student? A strip shows their level, the chords they have and the songs they're ready for.
  • Drag the lines between columns, or fold one away.

05 / 05 · The lesson builder

A handout to take home

Builder · Handout

The handout updates as you type: the chords, strums, a practice plan for the week and a note for parents.

  • Video links print as QR codes. Songs print a Scan to listen code.
  • Grey words are drafted for you; change any of them.
