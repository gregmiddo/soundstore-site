01 / 04 · Repairs & loans

Book a repair in

Repairs · Book in

The instrument gets a job number, a tag for the workshop and a claim slip for the customer.

  • Make, model, serial, colour, its condition and what was left with it: case, gig bag, strap, lead.
  • Pick a service from the price list and it sets the price.
  • Promised for puts it on the calendar.
  • Collecting it on a pickup run? Tick Not here yet and it waits for the trip.

02 / 04 · Repairs & loans

Every job, where it's up to

Repairs

Each job moves through booked in, waiting on parts, on the bench and ready, with colour on every row.

  • Parts used come off the stock count and onto the invoice.
  • Calls and notes kept on the job, so anyone can answer "is it ready?".
  • A faulty part goes back to the supplier straight from the job.

03 / 04 · Repairs & loans

Tell the customer it's ready

Repairs · Ready

Mark a job Ready and an email is written for you: check it and queue it, or let it go automatically.

  • Emails wait in the Email centre until they're sent.
  • The call or email is logged on the job.

04 / 04 · Repairs & loans

Loan gear while theirs is in

Loans · Lend something

Lend a guitar or amp in three steps: who, what and when it's due back, with a signed agreement.

  • Every piece of loan gear has its own code, serial, value, condition and history.
  • Gear that's out or being fixed is greyed out and says why.
  • Overdue loans show on Home and the calendar, with a reminder email one click away.
  • Scan the code when it comes back, check what came with it, and note its condition.
