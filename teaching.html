01 / 03 · Teaching the lesson

Teach mode

Builder · Teach

The part you're on is big in the middle, with a timer and a metronome at the part's tempo.

  • Done, next ticks a part off. The arrow keys move between parts.
  • Finish and give records the lesson with only the parts you did; the rest carry over next time.

02 / 03 · Teaching the lesson

Show it on the student's screen

Teach mode · Show to the student

A second window on a TV or the other monitor shows the student the part, big, with its timer and beat.

  • Teacher notes are never shown.
  • Between lessons it shows the shop's logo and the next lesson.

03 / 03 · Teaching the lesson

Record the lesson and who came

Lessons · Timetable

Click a lesson on the timetable and say what happened: attended, absent or cancelled.

  • What did they get today? marks the chords and strums they learned.
  • Recordings of the student playing are kept with the lesson.
