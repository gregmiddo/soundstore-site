01 / 07 · Point of sale

Take a sale in seconds

Point of sale · F2

Press F2 from anywhere and the cursor is already in the scan box. Scan each item, pick how they're paying, and click Finish sale.

  • Scan the same item again to add one more, or use − and + on the line.
  • No barcode? Type a few letters of the name or the SKU. Not in stock at all? Other item adds it with a price.
  • An instrument with a serial number asks for it as it's sold, so the receipt and warranty carry it.
  • Pay by Card, Cash, Gift voucher or Bank, or split one sale across several.
  • The sale is kept as you go: walk away mid-sale, or even close the app, and it's still there when you come back.

02 / 07 · Point of sale

Quick keys for the things you sell every day

Point of sale · Edit keys

Strings, picks, cables and repair labour are one click away on big coloured keys, grouped on tabs.

  • Each key shows its price and how many are left, in red when stock runs low.
  • Open-price keys for labour or room hire ask for the amount and what it's for.
  • Group keys open a whole stock category, like every set of strings.
  • The keys start themselves from your best sellers. Owners rename, recolour, reorder and add tabs.

03 / 07 · Point of sale

Discounts, quick cash and the customer card

Point of sale · pay side

Pick the customer and coloured chips show what matters before you take a cent.

  • Owes, Lay-by, Voucher, Repair ready, Order arrived and On loan chips, each one click from the record.
  • Discount the sale by percent or dollars, with a reason. Staff above the shop's limit need an Owner's PIN.
  • Cash in one click: Exact, $20, $50 or $100, with the change straight away.
  • A number pad for touch screens, switched on per PC.

04 / 07 · Point of sale

Park a sale, bring it back later

Point of sale · Park

A customer forgot their wallet or went to try another guitar? Park the sale and serve the next person.

  • Park as many sales as you need. Parked sales lists them, and Bring back puts one on the counter.
  • A sale already on the counter is parked first, so nothing is ever lost.
  • Switch person locks the screen so the next staff member signs in with their own PIN.

05 / 07 · Point of sale

Recent sales, reprints and refunds

Point of sale · Recent sales

Find any sale from today, or the last 90 days, reprint its receipt, or take something back.

  • Tick what's coming back and say if it's faulty, so good stock goes back on the shelf.
  • Give the money back the way they paid, or as store credit on a gift voucher.
  • Refunds over the shop's limit ask for an Owner's PIN.
  • Every refund is a proper credit note. Nothing is ever deleted.

06 / 07 · Point of sale

A screen that faces the customer

Point of sale · ⋯ · Show the customer screen

A second monitor shows the customer each item, any discount and the total as you scan.

  • It says Thank you! with their change after the sale.
  • Between sales it shows the shop's logo, or your flyers in turn.
  • It never shows costs, staff names, notes or account details.

07 / 07 · Point of sale

Close the day and back up

File · Close the day

At closing time one summary shows the day's takings, and one big button backs everything up.

  • A Z-read with the cash expected in the drawer, for the Owner.
  • Back up now makes a checked copy of everything, then it says when it's safe to close.
  • Exports for Xero or MYOB when you want them.
