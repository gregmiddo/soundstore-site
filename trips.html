Trip planner

The Trip planner opens on big buttons: plan a new trip, carry on, use a saved trip, or start from a suggestion.

02 / 04 · Trips & pickups

Plan a run on the map

Trip planner · Plan a trip

Tick the schools, venues and homes to visit, or click their dots on the map.

  • Shortest route from the shop and back, or drag stops into your own order.
  • Arrival windows for each stop, with an amber warning outside a place's hours.
  • Fuel and distance worked out as you go.
  • Nights away, with places to stay found near each town.

Pickups

Email every stop when you're coming, then print the run sheet or email it to the driver.

  • Repairs collected on the run are booked in as they come back.
  • A mileage log for the tax return.
