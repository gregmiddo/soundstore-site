Hire gear that can't be double-booked

Hire · New booking

PA, speakers, amps, mics and lights for gigs, weddings and venues, each piece on one booking at a time.

  • Day and weekend rates, and a bond for each piece.
  • Test and tag due dates: overdue gear can't be booked or go out.
  • Back-to-back bookings are fine; overlapping ones are refused.
  • A calendar of every piece by day.

02 / 05 · Hire, rent-to-buy & consignments

Out the door and back again

Hire · It's gone out · It's back

On pick-up day: the signed agreement, the bond, the ID you sighted and how the gear looks.

  • A late fee is suggested when it comes back late.
  • Damage charges, and the bond split between given back and kept.
  • Invoice it, and any bond kept pays toward the invoice.

03 / 05 · Hire, rent-to-buy & consignments

Rent-to-buy agreements

Rent-to-buy

A customer rents an instrument by the month and the rent counts toward buying it.

  • The agreement shows the rent paid, what's left to buy it out and when the next payment is due.
  • Each payment is a paid invoice. Anyone behind shows on Home.
  • Buy it out, or end it and take the instrument back.

04 / 05 · Hire, rent-to-buy & consignments

Consignments and the police register

Consignments

Sell second-hand gear for someone else: the shop keeps its commission and pays them the rest once it sells.

  • Photo ID checked and recorded for every second-hand item taken in.
  • The item goes on sale at the counter like any stock.
  • Export for the police saves the register as a spreadsheet when they ask.

05 / 05 · Hire, rent-to-buy & consignments

Gift vouchers with your own card

Vouchers

Sell a gift voucher with the shop's own card design, print it to cut out, or email it as a PDF.

  • Design the cards in the Flyers editor, with blanks filled in at the sale.
  • Double-click a voucher to see what's left on it and where it's been spent.
  • Take a voucher as payment at the counter by its code.
