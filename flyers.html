A flyer editor built into the shop

Flyers

Make promo flyers, gig posters and counter handouts without leaving the app.

  • Text, pictures, shapes, stickers, price tags and the shop's details that stay up to date.
  • Layers, freehand drawing, effects, and handles to resize, turn and group.
  • 283 built-in fonts, with favourites and look-alikes.
  • Pop it out into its own window on a second screen while the counter carries on.

A finished flyer in six steps

Flyers · New flyer

The New flyer wizard asks what it's for, where the details come from, the size, a look and the text, then makes it.

  • Templates for gig posters, what's on, lesson promos, sales and workshops.
  • The shop's brand kit of colours, fonts and text styles is offered first.

03 / 05 · Flyers, gig guides & events

Checked before it's printed

Flyers · Check

Design check looks for text too small or hard to read, things off the page, blurry pictures, sample text and spelling.

  • Print 1, 2 or 4 to a sheet with crop marks, or export a picture or PDF.
  • Make one design into many: another size, another layout, or one per row of a list.

04 / 05 · Flyers, gig guides & events

A gig guide that finds the gigs for you

Gig guide

One list of the gigs around town: your own events, gigs typed in, and venue calendars read from the web.

  • Choose how far around the shop and which dates, then tick the gigs you want.
  • Turn the ticked gigs into a gig-guide flyer with a live preview.
  • Email it to customers who agreed to news, or make square and story pictures for social media.

05 / 05 · Flyers, gig guides & events

Events and pop-up stalls

Events

Plan a market stall, school visit or gig, and see afterwards whether it was worth it.

  • A checklist, a pack list and the costs, with fuel worked out from the kilometres.
  • Sales and enquiries tagged to the event.
  • Profit after costs and your time, per hour, with the new customers it brought.
  • A gig poster for the event that stays up to date with it.
