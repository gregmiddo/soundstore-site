01 / 05 · Why SoundStore

Built for the independent music shop

Who it's for

SoundStore is designed around how an independent music shop works: the counter, the workshop, the teaching rooms and the van.

  • One app instead of a till, a spreadsheet, a diary and a design program.
  • Turn off the parts you don't use, and the menu shows only what you do.
  • Plain words on every screen, and a Visual Shop Guide with pictures for every task.

02 / 05 · Why SoundStore

Your PCs, your data

How it runs

SoundStore is a Windows desktop app. Your data lives on your own PC, not in someone else's cloud.

  • Works without the internet. The counter keeps selling when the connection drops.
  • Several counters in the shop on the same data, and travel laptops that sync in a signed file.
  • Australian GST, ABN, BAS-ready reports and Xero or MYOB exports.

03 / 05 · Why SoundStore

Nothing is ever deleted

Safe by design

Every sale, refund and change is kept with who did it. Mistakes are put right with a credit note, never erased.

  • Checked backups every day, in up to three places.
  • Owner PINs for refunds and discounts above the shop's limits.
