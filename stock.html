01 / 07 · Stock & suppliers

One stock list for everything you sell

Stock

Search by name, brand, SKU or barcode as you type, and see what's on hand, on order and what it cost.

  • Bring the whole list in from a spreadsheet, check it, and undo it if it isn't right.
  • Export to Excel, change hundreds of prices, and bring it back in.
  • Load a supplier's price list to update only the costs or prices of what you already stock.
  • Every change to the count is recorded with who did it and why.

02 / 07 · Stock & suppliers

Barcode and price labels

Stock · Print labels

Print labels with the name, price and barcode on A4 label sheets or a label printer.

  • Sheets of 21, 24, 30 or 65, with Start at label so a half-used sheet isn't wasted.
  • Print labels for everything that arrived on a delivery, straight from the purchase order.
  • EAN barcodes where the item has one, otherwise a Code 128 of the SKU.

03 / 07 · Stock & suppliers

Serial numbers and warranty

Stock · item

Tick an instrument as having a serial number and the sale asks for it.

  • The receipt and invoice show the serial and the warranty end date.
  • Warranty by item, by category (guitars 24 months, say) or the shop's default.
  • Book in a repair and Bought here finds the sale and its warranty from the serial.

04 / 07 · Stock & suppliers

Stocktakes that don't stop the shop

Stock · Stocktake

Print the counting sheet, type in what you counted and bring it back in.

  • Every difference is recorded, so you can see what went missing and when.
  • Carry on a stocktake over several days.

05 / 07 · Stock & suppliers

Purchase orders and special orders

Orders & POs

Order from a supplier with a numbered purchase order, email it, and tick it off when it arrives.

  • Receive part of an order and the rest stays open.
  • Special orders for customers, with a deposit that carries across to the invoice.
  • Order everything still to order makes one purchase order per supplier.
  • When it arrives the customer is flagged on Home until you've told them.

06 / 07 · Stock & suppliers

Suppliers, bills and returns

Suppliers

Keep track of what the shop owes, pay it off in parts, and send faulty stock back.

  • One line per supplier bill, with how urgent it is and the GST on it.
  • A return gets its own return note. Stock comes off when it's sent, and the supplier's credit comes off what you owe.
  • Look up opens the supplier's dealer site already searched for the product.

Price second-hand gear with confidence

Stock · Check a price

See what a used instrument sells for on Reverb, eBay and Google Shopping before you make a trade-in offer.

  • One click searches each site for that brand and model.
  • Add your own price-check sites in Settings › Links.
