Everything about a customer, in one place

Customers · Ctrl+F

Find anyone by name, phone or email and see their sales, repairs, hires, loans, lessons and what they owe.

  • Quick look on any search shows their card before you open it.
  • New sale, New repair and more straight from the card.
  • Instruments they bought, with serial numbers and warranty.

02 / 05 · Customers, enquiries & invoices

Enquiries you never forget

Enquiries

Note who asked for what, and the day you said you'd get back to them.

  • Every call, message or visit is logged with the next time to try.
  • Reminders on Home, the calendar, the menu and when you sign in.
  • Turn into a quote, special order, repair, hire or new student in one click, filled in from the enquiry.
  • A stock watch tells you when what they asked for comes in.

03 / 05 · Customers, enquiries & invoices

Invoices and quotes

Invoices

For schools, account customers and anyone who wants a proper invoice, with your logo, terms and bank details.

  • Take part payments by card, cash, bank transfer or gift voucher.
  • Quotes that turn into invoices.
  • Credit notes for anything that comes back. Nothing is ever deleted.

Invoices · Tell customers

A friendly reminder with their statement attached, for one invoice or everyone overdue at once.
